Gameday Pulse

Your fantasy football league, brought to life. Follow ESPN and Sleeper league matchups, player scoring updates, NFL games, and animated plays.

Support

Email chase@sudokuhero.app for help. Include your app version, device model, and a description of what happened. Do not send passwords, session cookies, connection tokens, or payment details.

Getting started

  • Choose Connect ESPN or Connect Sleeper and follow the connection instructions. Yahoo connections are not currently available.
  • To explore without connecting a league, choose Explore sample league. Its scores and plays are simulated.
  • Use Settings to choose your team, adjust animations and haptics, or manage connected leagues.

Remove Ads and restore purchases

In the iOS release with advertising, open Settings > Remove Ads for a one-time purchase. There is no subscription. To recover an existing purchase, use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Privacy choices

Open Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap Privacy to expand it. Basic usage statistics defaults on for new installations when available, with a launch notice and an off switch. Previous opt-outs are respected. Detailed analytics stays off until you opt in. Neither choice affects access to the app.

Read the Gameday Pulse privacy policy for details and privacy-request instructions.
