Gameday Pulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes how Gameday Pulse handles information. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean Chase Friedman, the developer of Gameday Pulse. For support or privacy requests, contact chase@sudokuhero.app.

Current and planned services: Yahoo integration is not yet released. Google AdMob banner ads, Amplitude analytics, and Sentry crash reporting are planned for future updates and are not currently integrated. The future-service sections below do not describe collection already occurring through those services.

Fantasy leagues and provider connections

Gameday Pulse processes fantasy-league information such as league and team identifiers, team names, rosters, matchups, standings, player statistics, scores, and available projections. It uses this information to display your selected leagues, follow matchups, and generate scoring updates and animations. You do not need a separate Gameday Pulse account. The built-in sample league uses simulated data and can be explored without connecting a personal fantasy account.

ESPN

When you sign in through the app’s embedded browser, your password is entered on ESPN’s website. The app reads the ESPN session cookies needed to access private leagues, including SWID and espn_s2, and saves connection information using secure storage on native devices. Private ESPN score requests are made directly from the native app to ESPN. The embedded sign-in browser also maintains its own session storage. Public leagues can be accessed without a private-league sign-in.

Sleeper and public sports data

Sleeper connections use a league identifier, league link, or public username lookup. They do not require a Sleeper password. League and player information is requested from Sleeper’s public services. Public ESPN services also supply NFL game context and related information. Providers may receive technical request information, including your IP address, when the app or embedded browser connects to them. Their own privacy policies apply to information they collect.

Optional browser-preview helper

The optional computer helper for browser previews reads ESPN data on the computer running it and sends league information to paired viewers. Private ESPN session values are used by that helper rather than sent to viewers. This helper is separate from the native phone connection. Its form-entered sessions are held in memory and are cleared when the helper restarts; manually configured credentials are controlled by the person running the helper.

Information stored on your device

The app stores league selections, team and watch preferences, local scoring and notification history, read receipts, and replay-related state on your device. Native connection credentials use secure device storage. Browser previews use browser storage, with connection credentials held in session storage. The operating system may include some app data in backups according to your settings.

Diagnostics and support

The optional live-test log records technical timing and error information, scoring changes, player names, roster membership, and animation events. It is stored locally and can persist when you reopen the app. Recording this log does not automatically upload it. If you choose to share a log, the recipient receives the exported information. These logs are not described as anonymous.

If you email us or send a diagnostic report, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use correspondence to respond, troubleshoot, and handle privacy requests. Email is processed by the services used to send and receive it. Please do not send passwords, provider session cookies, or connection tokens.

Planned Yahoo integration

Yahoo integration is not yet released. Its planned design uses Yahoo sign-in and a Cloudflare-hosted service to obtain read-only fantasy access, handle authorization tokens, and retrieve league information. Before introducing it, we will update this policy to describe the actual data processed, server storage and retention, disconnect behavior, and provider choices. No Yahoo functionality or current Yahoo collection is implied by this notice.

Planned advertising and privacy choices

We plan to introduce Google AdMob banner advertisements in a future update. If enabled, Google and advertising partners may process IP addresses indicating approximate location, device and app identifiers (including advertising identifiers when permitted), app and ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information, and consent choices for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Personalized advertising would depend on applicable permissions and consent. On iOS and iPadOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission is separate from regional advertising choices presented through Google’s User Messaging Platform. Permission through one mechanism does not override choices through the other. Where required, those choices would be obtained before the relevant advertising processing begins.

Declining tracking does not necessarily eliminate all advertising-related processing. Non-personalized or limited ads can still involve data collection and device storage for delivery, frequency limits, reporting, and fraud prevention. Depending on the configuration and choices, a future version may serve non-personalized or limited ads, or no ads. Advertising choices are not a general analytics or crash-reporting opt-out.

Before ads are introduced, we will update this policy with the actual implementation, advertising partners, and controls available in that release. Advertising settings and permission prompts described as planned are not represented as existing features.

Planned analytics and crash reporting

Amplitude product analytics and Sentry crash reporting are planned for a future update. Neither is currently integrated into Gameday Pulse. Before enabling them, we will describe the actual events and diagnostic data sent, identifiers, purposes, retention arrangements, and available choices. We do not claim that another app’s SDK settings, data filters, or opt-out controls apply to Gameday Pulse.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local information remains until removed, replaced, or cleared through available app or device controls. Disconnect controls remove the saved connection they manage. The native ESPN session-clearing flow deletes its saved session and clears the app’s embedded ESPN browser session; it does not sign you out of Safari, Chrome, or your ESPN account elsewhere.

Secure storage on iOS uses the Keychain and may survive uninstalling the app. Do not rely on uninstalling alone to remove every credential. Use the app’s disconnect controls and your provider’s account or session controls where appropriate. Device backups may retain copies separately.

Removing local data or logs does not delete copies you previously shared, support correspondence, or records held by fantasy providers or other services. We retain correspondence as needed to respond and handle related requests. Provider records and retention are governed by their policies and applicable arrangements. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Privacy requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to processing. Email chase@sudokuhero.app with requests concerning information held by us. We may need information to locate the relevant records and verify your request. For information controlled by ESPN, Sleeper, or another provider, use that provider’s privacy controls or request channels. You may also contact your local data protection authority.
